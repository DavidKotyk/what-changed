## Baseline on the demonstration

The simple line diff produces 4 added and 7 removed lines. This includes title, brand copy, a removed section and changed example wording. What Changed? presents 4 procedure steps: 1 review change, 1 text unchanged, 2 uncertain. The diff and step labels measure different things; no numerical accuracy or time-saving comparison is justified. Raw diff remains available because it exposes changes outside the checklist.

## Known failures retained in the evaluation

- **Cross-reference change outside anchor:** No cross-reference resolution: anchored passage unchanged although its referenced deadline changed. Desired changed; actual unchanged.
- **Implicit hypothetical without trigger words:** The small example-language heuristic misses this hypothetical phrasing. Desired uncertain; actual changed.

## Performance scope

Diagnostic cases execute locally in Node. Measured elapsed times are in evaluation.json and vary by hardware/process. These small fixtures do not establish worst-case latency, memory, or usability. No model, network request, key or paid service is involved.
