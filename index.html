Section anchors and shared words locate passages. The app compares their text; it does not understand policy or rewrite your procedure. “Text unchanged” refers to the passage, not the correctness of your instruction.

What this can miss

Synonyms, renamed sections, cross-references, embedded exceptions and long sections can produce weak or misleading matches. Example-language warnings are heuristic. Read both passages and the full source before acting. English plain text and Markdown only.
